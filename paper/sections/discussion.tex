\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

Thresholds selected on point estimates or percentile-bootstrap bounds often claimed more evidence than held. Because these are call-averaged ratios, not the local ratios of ClinGen
calibrations, this does not show that published PP3/BP4 thresholds are wrong, only that point estimates and bootstrap
bounds can overstate evidence at single-gene sample sizes. Bounds built as confidence intervals for the ratio, exact or
Katz, held in at least 97.9\% of draws; the exact certificate is the conservative choice that keeps a guarantee at every
sample size. \textsc{CertiVar} makes such claims checkable without an estimate of the share of damaging variants, and
its mixture check says when a target set lies outside what the assay data can certify. It applies to genes with a
functional assay: to audit whether a predictor's claimed evidence strength holds against the assay, and to call the
variants the assay missed at assayed residues (147 ClinVar VUS here), for predictors whose scores pass the check. For the
4,010 unassayed BRCA1 and BRCA2 VUS at other residues, and for AlphaMissense and REVEL in part of the MSH2 samples, the
check declined. The cost is conservatism: certified evidence was mostly supporting-level, and PAC
thresholds abstained on 58\% to 82\% of variants.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The labels are assay classes, not clinical pathogenicity \cite{Brnich2020}, and the ACMG/AMP levels are reused here as a
reporting scale; clinical use requires an assay validated against clinical classifications, as its authors did for BRCA1
\cite{Findlay2018} and, with their own classes, for BRCA2 \cite{Huang2025}. The BRCA2 score set has no deposited classes,
so our BRCA2 labels come from the assay's controls (sensitivity in \cref{tab:app_labels}). A certified level describes the
calls as a class and weights residues equally, so a borderline call can carry less evidence than its class. The
guarantees assume residues exchangeable with the calibrated ones; they do not cover intermediate, splice-region or
out-of-region variants. Intermediate variants are a hazard, because whether a VUS would score intermediate is unknown
before it is assayed, and 19.6\% of them were called LoF. The mixture check tests only a necessary condition and missed
most VUS shifts. Results cover five
cancer-predisposition genes, four of them in DNA repair. None of the predictors was trained on these assay labels, but
REVEL was trained on labelled variants and the AlphaMissense classes were set on ClinVar, so both may overlap our
evaluation sets \cite{Ioannidis2016,Grimm2015}; ESM-2, trained without variant labels, gave the same coverage and
validity.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\textsc{CertiVar} turns a variant-effect predictor and a few hundred assayed residues of a gene into LoF, functional or
no calls with finite-sample guarantees on per-class error rates and on call-averaged likelihood ratios for the assay
after threshold selection, without an estimate of how common damaging variants are, and checks whether a target set can
inherit them. Code, data and results (release v1.1.0):\newline\url{https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/certivar}.
