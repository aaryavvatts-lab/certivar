\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

Thresholds selected on point estimates or percentile-bootstrap bounds often claimed more evidence than held. Because
these are call-averaged ratios, not the local ratios of ClinGen calibrations, this does not show that published PP3/BP4
thresholds are wrong, only that point estimates and bootstrap bounds can overstate evidence at single-gene sample sizes.
\textsc{CertiVar} makes the claim checkable without an estimate of the share of damaging variants. Its intended use is a
gene with a functional assay: calling variants the assay missed within assayed regions, and checking whether a predictor's claimed evidence strength holds against the assay.
\Cref{s:extra} counts the ClinVar VUS that receive certified calls, tests the invariance assumption on unlabelled
targets and adds Katz and pseudocount-bootstrap baselines. The cost is conservatism: certified evidence was mostly
supporting-level, and PAC thresholds abstained on 58\% to 82\% of variants.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The labels are assay classes, not clinical pathogenicity \cite{Brnich2020}; clinical use requires an assay validated
against clinical classifications, as its authors did for BRCA1 \cite{Findlay2018} and, with their own classes rather
than our control-based labels, for BRCA2 \cite{Huang2025}. A certified level describes the calls as a class, so a
borderline call can carry less evidence than its class. The guarantees assume residues exchangeable with the calibrated
ones; they do not cover intermediate, splice-region or out-of-region variants, and per-class coverage after selection is
guaranteed only at $1-\max G$. Results cover five cancer-predisposition genes, four of them in DNA repair. REVEL was
trained on labelled variants and the AlphaMissense classes were set on ClinVar, so both may overlap our evaluation sets
\cite{Ioannidis2016,Grimm2015}; ESM-2, trained without variant labels, gave similar coverage and validity.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\textsc{CertiVar} turns a variant-effect predictor and a few hundred assayed residues of a gene into LoF, functional or
no calls with finite-sample guarantees on per-class error rates (in expectation, at a fixed miscoverage level) and on call-averaged
likelihood ratios with respect to the assay after threshold selection, without an estimate of how common damaging
variants are. Code and data (release v1.2.3):\newline\url{https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/certivar}.
