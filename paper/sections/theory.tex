\subsection{Guarantees}\label{sec:theory}

Let $Q$ be the law of a variant obtained by drawing a residue from the gene's population of assayed residues and then
one of its variants uniformly at random (binary-labelled, non-splice variants outside the ClinVar labelled set).

\begin{assumption}[Clustered sampling]\label{as:cluster}
Residues in $\mathcal A$, in $\mathcal B$ and of a test variant are independent draws from one residue population;
within a residue, the kept or tested variant is chosen uniformly at random; $q$ does not depend on these data.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Class-conditional invariance]\label{as:shift}
Target variants have, for $k\in\{0,1\}$, the same law of $q$ given $Y=k$ as a test variant under
Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}; their LoF share $\pi=\Pr(Y=1)$ may differ.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Per-class coverage]\label{prop:mondrian}
Under Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}, for a fixed $\alpha_k$, with the in-expectation rank and conditionally on $n_k$,
$1-\alpha_k\le\Pr(k\in C\mid Y=k,n_k)\le 1-\alpha_k+1/(n_k+1)$, the upper bound holding when there are no ties.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[High-probability per-class coverage]\label{prop:pac}
Under Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}, for a fixed $\alpha_k$ and with the PAC rank, $\Pr(k\in C\mid Y=k,\hat t_k)\ge1-\alpha_k$
with probability at least $1-\delta$ over the calibration data for each $k$, and for both classes with probability at
least $1-2\delta$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Coverage after selection]\label{prop:select}
Under Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}, for any $\hat\alpha\in G$ chosen from data independent of the test variant
(for example by \cref{alg:certivar}), the in-expectation rank gives $\Pr(k\in C\mid Y=k)\ge1-\max G$; the PAC rank at
level $\delta/|G|$ gives $\Pr(k\in C\mid Y=k,\hat t_k)\ge1-\hat\alpha$ for each $k$ with probability at least $1-\delta$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Certified likelihood ratios]\label{thm:lr}
Under Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}, conditionally on $\mathcal A$ and with probability at least $1-\delta$ over
$\mathcal B$, $\mathrm{LR}^+(\hat t(\alpha))\ge L^+(\alpha)$ under $Q$ for every $\alpha\in G$ simultaneously; the same
holds for $\mathrm{LR}^-\le U^-$ with a second $\delta$. Because the event does not depend on the level, each call type
returned by \cref{alg:certivar} reaches its certified level at every evidence level reported, with probability at least
$1-\delta$ (both call types with probability at least $1-2\delta$), whichever $\alpha$ is selected.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-sequence certification]\label{thm:fs}
Under Assumption~\ref{as:cluster}, fix a level $\tau$ and an order of $G$ chosen in advance. Reject $H_\alpha:
\mathrm{LR}^+(\hat t(\alpha))\le\tau$ when $\ell_{\mathrm{TP}}/u_{\mathrm{FP}}>\tau$ with both endpoints at level
$\delta/2$, stop at the first $\alpha$ not rejected, and select the rejected $\alpha$ (a prefix of the order) with the largest yield. Then,
conditionally on $\mathcal A$, the selected LoF calls have $\mathrm{LR}^+>\tau$ with probability at least $1-\delta$.
The same holds for functional calls with $u_{\mathrm{FN}}/\ell_{\mathrm{TN}}<\tau'$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[No prior needed]\label{cor:shift}
Under Assumptions~\ref{as:cluster} and~\ref{as:shift}, Propositions~\ref{prop:mondrian}--\ref{prop:select} and
Theorems~\ref{thm:lr}--\ref{thm:fs} hold for target variants whatever their LoF share $\pi\in(0,1)$: for a fixed
$\alpha$, LoF variants are called functional at rate at most $\alpha_1$, and the certified likelihood ratios with
respect to the assay outcome need no prior.
\end{corollary}

Propositions~\ref{prop:mondrian} and~\ref{prop:pac} are the class-conditional and training-conditional conformal
guarantees \cite{Vovk2005,Sadinle2019,Vovk2013,Tong2018}, here for residue-clustered data. Proposition~\ref{prop:select}
holds because both ranks in \eqref{eq:rank} are non-increasing in $\alpha$, so coverage at any selected $\alpha$ is at
least coverage at $\max G$. Theorem~\ref{thm:lr} follows the risk-control template \cite{Bates2021rcps}: given
$\mathcal A$ and the labels in $\mathcal B$, the four counts are binomial, and a union bound over the $2|G|$
endpoints covers every $\alpha$, including a selected one. Theorem~\ref{thm:fs} follows Learn-then-Test
\cite{Angelopoulos2025ltt}: a true $H_\alpha$ is rejected only if an endpoint fails, and a false claim requires
rejecting the first true hypothesis in the order. Proofs are in Supplement~S1.

\paragraph{Scope.}
After selection, per-class coverage is guaranteed only at $1-\max G$ with the in-expectation rank; we report the coverage
achieved in \cref{sec:evidence}. Theorem~\ref{thm:fs} certifies one level at a time and gives no numerical bound.
Assumption~\ref{as:cluster} treats assayed residues as exchangeable, which a contiguous region need not be;
Assumption~\ref{as:shift} holds only approximately for VUS, on which AlphaMissense discriminates less well
(\cref{sec:data}), and not for variants whose assay result would be intermediate.
