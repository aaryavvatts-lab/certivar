\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Calibrating predictors to clinical evidence.}
ClinGen maps predictor scores to ACMG/AMP evidence strengths through local likelihood ratios estimated against ClinVar
labels, with a one-sided bootstrap bound and a prior estimated by a positive--unlabelled method \cite{Pejaver2022}; the
approach has been extended to more predictors \cite{Bergquist2025}, to assay scores \cite{Brnich2020,Fayer2021,Zeiberg2025},
and to gene- and domain-specific calibration \cite{Tejura2024,Chen2026,Badonyi2025}. Where these report uncertainty, it
comes from resampling: the bootstrap \cite{Pejaver2022,Bergquist2025} or sign-test bounds on the median of resampled
likelihood ratios \cite{Badonyi2025}. Such bounds are approximate at small sample sizes and are not adjusted for the
choice of a threshold from the same data.

\paragraph{Conformal prediction, selective classification and risk control.}
Split conformal prediction gives finite-sample coverage \cite{Vovk2005}; its class-conditional (Mondrian) form controls
coverage within each class \cite{Vovk2005,Sadinle2019}; order-statistic thresholds give high-probability,
training-conditional control \cite{Vovk2013,Tong2018}; and keeping one member per group handles clustered data
\cite{Dunn2023}. Abstaining classifiers trade the share of inputs they classify against error \cite{Chow1970,Geifman2017}.
Selecting a threshold from a grid with binomial bounds and a multiplicity correction, or by fixed-sequence testing, is
the template of risk-controlling prediction sets and Learn-then-Test \cite{Bates2021rcps,Angelopoulos2025ltt}. Harrizi
et al.\ reported split-conformal prediction sets for missense predictors on ClinVar benchmarks at 90\% marginal coverage,
treating a set with both labels as a refusal to call \cite{Harrizi2026}; \textsc{CertiVar} instead controls coverage
within each class, calibrates on the gene's assay labels and certifies the likelihood ratio of the calls. Our
certificate divides two exact one-sided Clopper--Pearson endpoints; score-type and log-scale intervals for a ratio of
binomial proportions \cite{Katz1978,Koopman1984} are usually narrower but only asymptotically valid, which matters at the
counts of a single gene. The building blocks are known; what is new is their combination into a certificate on the
OddsPath scale for residue-clustered assay labels, with a coverage guarantee after threshold selection, a
fixed-sequence variant, and a known-truth benchmark of how often point estimates and bootstrap bounds overstate
evidence.

\paragraph{Label shift.}
Label-shift methods adjust posteriors or conformal weights to an estimated target prior
\cite{Saerens2002,Lipton2018,Podkopaev2021}. Class-conditional quantities do not depend on the prior by construction;
the difficulty is that the target must satisfy class-conditional invariance (Assumption~\ref{as:shift}), which we test.
