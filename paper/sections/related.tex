\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

ClinGen maps predictor scores to ACMG/AMP evidence strengths through local likelihood ratios estimated against ClinVar
labels, with a one-sided bootstrap bound \cite{Pejaver2022}; the approach has been extended to more predictors
\cite{Bergquist2025}, to assay scores \cite{Brnich2020,Fayer2021,Zeiberg2025} and to gene- and domain-specific
calibration \cite{Tejura2024,Chen2026,Badonyi2025}, with uncertainty from the bootstrap \cite{Pejaver2022,Bergquist2025}
or sign-test bounds on resampled likelihood ratios \cite{Badonyi2025}. Split conformal prediction gives finite-sample
coverage \cite{Vovk2005}; its class-conditional (Mondrian) form controls coverage within each class
\cite{Vovk2005,Sadinle2019}, and clustered conformal prediction pools classes with similar scores when each class has
few labels \cite{Ding2023}; order-statistic thresholds give training-conditional control \cite{Vovk2013,Tong2018,Park2020};
keeping one member per group handles clustered data \cite{Dunn2023}; and abstaining classifiers trade the share of
inputs classified against error \cite{Chow1970}. Choosing a threshold from candidates with exact binomial tail bounds and
a union bound is the basis of selective classification with guaranteed risk \cite{Geifman2017} and of
risk-controlling prediction sets \cite{Bates2021rcps}; fixed-sequence testing over an ordered grid is the Learn-then-Test
template \cite{Angelopoulos2025ltt}. Harrizi et al.\ reported split-conformal prediction sets for missense predictors on
ClinVar benchmarks at 90\% coverage \cite{Harrizi2026}; \textsc{CertiVar} instead controls coverage within each class,
calibrates on the gene's assay labels and certifies the likelihood ratio of the calls. Large-sample intervals for a ratio
of binomial proportions \cite{Katz1978,Koopman1984} are narrower than our ratio of exact endpoints but carry no
finite-sample guarantee; \cref{sec:evidence} compares the Katz interval. Label-shift methods adjust posteriors or conformal weights to an estimated
target prior \cite{Saerens2002,Lipton2018,Podkopaev2021}; class-conditional quantities need no prior, provided the target
satisfies class-conditional invariance (Assumption~\ref{as:shift}), which \cref{sec:method} tests. Relative to these methods,
\textsc{CertiVar} certifies a ratio of class-conditional call rates for both call types of an abstaining rule, on
residue-clustered assay labels and on the OddsPath scale, tests the invariance it relies on, and measures against known
population likelihood ratios how often point estimates and bootstrap bounds overstate evidence.
