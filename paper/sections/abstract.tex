\begin{center}\textbf{Abstract}\end{center}
\noindent Variant-effect predictors are a common source of evidence for missense variants of uncertain significance (VUS),
and the Bayesian form of the ACMG/AMP guidelines expresses evidence strength as a likelihood ratio. Gene-specific
calibrations estimate this ratio from few labelled variants, as point estimates or resampling-based bounds that are not designed to remain valid once a threshold has been chosen
from the same data. We present \textsc{CertiVar}, which
turns a predictor score into loss-of-function (LoF) calls, functional calls or abstentions using class-conditional
conformal thresholds learned from saturation genome editing or deep mutational scanning data, with one variant per residue. It then certifies a bound on the
likelihood ratio of each call type with respect to the assay outcome that stays valid after a threshold is chosen from a
grid, by a Bonferroni correction, or certifies evidence levels by fixed-sequence testing. Because these
guarantees condition on the assay class, none needs the unknown share of LoF variants among the variants being called, provided their
within-class score distribution matches that of the assayed variants.
On 14,477 assayed missense variants in BRCA1, BRCA2, BAP1, RAD51C and MSH2, scored by AlphaMissense, REVEL and ESM-2,
mean worst-class coverage over 50 splits was 0.896--0.908 at a 0.90 target in all 15 gene--predictor pairs and stayed at 0.89 or above when
the LoF share of the calibration data ranged from 5\% to 50\%; marginal conformal prediction calibrated at a 5\% share
covered LoF variants at only 0.11 in its worst pair. In resampling experiments with known population likelihood ratios,
all certified bounds of a draw held in 99.8\% of draws (guaranteed: at least 90\%), against 56.5\% for
percentile-bootstrap bounds, and fixed-sequence testing, which certifies one evidence level at a time, more than doubled
the certification of moderate LoF evidence. The certificates concern the assay, not pathogenicity; certified evidence was mostly
supporting-level and held for 88.8\% of certified bounds in held-out protein regions, below the nominal 95\% per call type. Code:
\url{https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/certivar}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} variant effect prediction; conformal prediction; likelihood ratio; ACMG/AMP
evidence calibration; saturation genome editing; deep mutational scanning; variants of uncertain significance;
multiple testing.
