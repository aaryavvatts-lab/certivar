\begin{center}\textbf{Abstract}\end{center}
\noindent Variant-effect predictors are a major source of evidence for missense variants of uncertain significance,
and the Bayesian form of the ACMG/AMP guidelines expresses evidence strength as a likelihood ratio. Gene-specific
calibrations estimate this ratio from few labelled variants, with point estimates or resampling-based bounds that carry
no finite-sample guarantee once a threshold has been chosen from the same data. We present \textsc{CertiVar}, which
turns a predictor score into loss-of-function (LoF) calls, functional calls or abstentions using class-conditional
conformal thresholds learned from saturation genome editing or deep mutational scanning data, with one variant per
residue. It then certifies a bound on the likelihood ratio of each call type with respect to the assay outcome that
stays valid after a threshold is chosen from a grid, by a Bonferroni correction or fixed-sequence testing. Because these
guarantees condition on the assay class, none needs the unknown share of LoF variants among the variants being called, provided their
within-class score distribution matches the assayed ones.
On 14,477 assayed missense variants in BRCA1, BRCA2, BAP1, RAD51C and MSH2, scored by AlphaMissense, REVEL and ESM-2,
worst-class coverage was 0.896--0.908 at a 0.90 target in all 15 gene--predictor pairs and stayed at 0.89 or above when
the LoF share of the calibration data ranged from 5\% to 50\%, where marginal conformal prediction fell to 0.11. In
resampling experiments with known true likelihood ratios, every certified bound in a draw held in 99.8\% of draws,
against 56.5\% for percentile-bootstrap bounds, and fixed-sequence testing more than doubled the certification of
moderate LoF evidence. The certificates concern the assay, not pathogenicity; certified evidence was mostly
supporting-level and held in 88.8\% of cases in held-out protein regions, below the nominal 95\%. Code:
\url{https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/certivar}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} variant effect prediction; conformal prediction; likelihood ratio; ACMG/AMP
evidence calibration; saturation genome editing; deep mutational scanning; variants of uncertain significance;
multiple testing.
