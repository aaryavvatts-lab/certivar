\begin{center}\textbf{Abstract}\end{center}
\noindent Variant-effect predictors are a major source of evidence for missense variants of uncertain significance,
and the Bayesian form of the ACMG/AMP guidelines expresses evidence strength as a likelihood ratio.
Gene-specific calibrations estimate this ratio from few labelled variants, as point estimates or resampling-based
bounds that carry no finite-sample guarantee once a threshold has been chosen from the same data. We present
\textsc{CertiVar}, which turns a predictor score into loss-of-function (LoF) calls, functional calls or abstentions
using class-conditional conformal thresholds learned from saturation genome editing or deep mutational scanning data,
with one variant per residue because substitutions at a residue are dependent. It then certifies a
bound on the likelihood ratio of each call type with respect to the assay outcome, using a Bonferroni correction or
fixed-sequence testing so that the bound stays valid after a threshold is chosen from a grid. Every guarantee conditions on the
assay class (per-class coverage at a fixed miscoverage level), so none requires the unknown share of LoF variants among the variants being called, provided that their
within-class score distribution matches that of the assayed variants. On 14,477 assayed
missense variants in BRCA1, BRCA2, BAP1, RAD51C and MSH2, scored by AlphaMissense, REVEL and ESM-2, worst-class coverage
was 0.896--0.908 at a 0.90 target in all 15 gene--predictor pairs, and stayed at 0.89 or above when the LoF share of
the calibration data was moved from 5\% to 50\%; marginal conformal prediction calibrated at a 5\% share covered LoF
variants at only 0.11 in its worst pair. In resampling experiments
with known true likelihood ratios, every certified bound in a draw held in 99.8\% of draws, against 56.5\% for
percentile-bootstrap bounds, and fixed-sequence testing more than doubled the certification of moderate LoF evidence.
The certificates concern the assay, not pathogenicity; certified evidence was mostly supporting-level, needed same-gene
assay data, and held in 88.8\% of cases in held-out protein regions, below the nominal 95\%. Code:
\url{https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/certivar}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} variant effect prediction; conformal prediction; likelihood ratio; ACMG/AMP
evidence calibration; saturation genome editing; deep mutational scanning; variants of uncertain significance;
multiple testing.
